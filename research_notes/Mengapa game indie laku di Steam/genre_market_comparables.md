# Steam market for games comparable to Gatewarden (horde-survivor / roguelite tower-defense / auto-battler), as of 27 Nov 2026

Method notes for the report writer:
- "Steam data, 27 Nov 2026" = pulled by me on 2026-11-27 from Steam's public store endpoints (`api/appdetails` for price/release date/developer, `appreviewhistogram` for summed positive+negative reviews, all languages). Prices are **current US list prices, not launch prices**. Release dates are the **1.0 date** shown on the store; Early Access (EA) start dates are given separately only where a source states them.
- Anything labelled "estimate" is a third-party model (Gamalytic, VG Insights, games-stats.com etc.), not a developer figure.
- Some findings come only from search-result snippets that I did not open in full; these are marked "(snippet only)".

## 1. Which Steam games are the closest comparables, and how did they perform?

### Takeaway
The hits in all three neighbouring genres have tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands of reviews and (where disclosed) 0.5M to 1M+ copies, and almost all were made by teams of 1 to ~10; but the titles closest to Gatewarden's actual mix (hero + horde + defend-a-base + recruit units) sit in the mid-tier of roughly 1,000 to 10,000 reviews. No comparable in the list combines a flat-vector look, synthesized audio and a browser origin with a large Steam result after 2022.

### Inferences
- Gatewarden's nearest structural neighbours are Brotato (waves + shop between waves), Thronefall and The King is Watching (defend a centre against waves), Monsters are Coming! (survivor + defend a structure), Just King (hero + auto-battler party vs hordes) and BALL x PIT / DRG: Survivor (survivor plus a second system). The "survivor + something" hybrid is a proven pattern at the top end, but the two hybrids closest in scale (Monsters are Coming! at 78% positive / ~2,900 reviews with a publisher, Just King at ~1,400 reviews) landed in the mid-tier, which is a more realistic ceiling to plan around than the million-sellers.

- China is a large share of sales for two of the comparables with disclosed splits (The King is Watching >30%, Backpack Battles 48%), which suggests Simplified Chinese localization matters in these genres.

## 2. How crowded is the bullet-heaven / survivors-like genre in 2024-2026, and what do analysts say?

### Takeaway
Supply has exploded (69 tagged releases in 2024, 103 in 2025, 329 in the first nine months of 2026, with 753 more announced), yet the genre still clears review thresholds about three times as often as Steam overall; analysts say it remains viable for small teams only at a clearly higher quality bar and roughly a year or more of work.

### Cited Findings
- Games tagged Bullet Heaven on Steam as of 27 Sep 2026: 1,318 pages, 565 released. Releases per year: 2022: 17; 2023: 37; 2024: 69; 2025: 103; 2026 to 27 Sep: 329 versus 65 in the same weeks of 2025 (+406%). 753 pages are unreleased, 102 of them dated within 90 days. The site warns the growth is partly inflated by Steam's May 2026 tag overhaul (Bullet Heaven became an official tag). — [SteamPageAnalyzer, Sep 2026](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven)
- Cohort of 107 Bullet Heaven games released 1 Jan 2024 to 30 Jun 2025: 88.8% reached 10 reviews, 41.1% reached 100 reviews, 13.1% reached 1,000 reviews (95% range 8% to 20.8%); median 75 reviews; median list price $5.99. All of Steam in the same window (24,918 games): 53.2% / 17.3% / 4.2%, median 11 reviews, median price $4.99. — [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven)
- Same cohort by price: under $5 (46 games) 23.9% reached 100 reviews and 8.7% reached 1,000, median 37 reviews; $5 to $9.99 (42 games) 52.4% reached 100 and 14.3% reached 1,000, median 141 reviews. Groups above $10 were too small to compare. — [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven)
- The broader Action Roguelike tag is far more crowded and has ordinary odds: 1,209 releases in 2024, 1,384 in 2025, 1,505 in 2026 to 27 Sep; in the Jan 2024 to Jun 2025 cohort (1,849 games) 16.8% reached 100 reviews and 3.5% reached 1,000; median 11 reviews. Under $5 (869 games): 7.2% / 0.8%; $5 to $9.99: 23.2% / 4.8%; $10 to $14.99: 36.3% / 4.8%; $15 to $19.99: 57% / 24.1%. — [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/action-roguelike)
- Tower Defense as a sub-tag inside Action Roguelike (61 games, 2024 to mid-2025): 19.7% reached 100 reviews, 3.3% reached 1,000 (95% range 0.9% to 11.2%); 185 unreleased pages. — [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/action-roguelike)
- Most-reviewed Bullet Heaven releases between 27 Sep 2025 and 27 Sep 2026: BALL x PIT ($14.99), Yet Another Zombie Survivors ($14.99), The Spell Brigade ($12.99), Pathogenic ($9.99), Arms of God ($11.50), Hell Maiden ($9.99), Vital Shell ($5.99), Tower of Babel: Survivors of Chaos ($12.99). — [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven)
- How To Market A Game (Chris Zukowski), May 2026: "Vital Shell shows that Bullet Heaven is still a viable genre for small teams" but "it is no longer a quick-win-rapid-release genre"; "The Bullet Heaven genre is more competitive and so the quality bar is much higher"; a year or so of development is probably the minimum. — [How To Market A Game, 19 May 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/19/2026-state-of-bullet-heavens-how-vital-shell-succeeded/)
- Steam-wide baseline from Gamalytic's 2025 analysis: median game revenue $249 in 2025 ($222 in 2024); 66% of games earned under $1,000 and 9.9% earned $50,000 or more (snippet only). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/analysts-report-drop-in-game-revenue-on-steam-despite-growing-number-of-releases)
- Roguelike/roguelite releases on Steam rose from 432 in 2021 to 1,926 in 2025 (snippet only). — [Recognizing Patterns: "The roguelite cull hasn't happened"](https://recognizingpatterns.substack.com/p/the-roguelite-cull-hasnt-happened)
- Vampire Survivors' creator Luca Galante has called his success luck and said everyone else now has to "stand out among the hundreds" (snippet only). — [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/games/roguelike/vampire-survivors-creator-admits-his-success-was-lucky-amid-a-wave-of-cheap-mobile-knock-offs-for-everyone-else-they-have-to-stand-out-among-the-hundreds)
- Valve made Bullet Heaven an official tag in 2026 and a Steam Bullet Fest 2026 event followed a community campaign (snippet only). — [Rogueliker](https://rogueliker.com/bullet-heaven-fest-steam-tags/); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/steam-bullet-fest-best-vampire-survivors-sektori-gungeon-2000704325)

### Inferences
- The 41% / 13% hit rates describe the 2024 to mid-2025 cohort, when there were about 70 to 100 releases a year. With 329 releases in nine months of 2026 and 753 more queued, the same audience is now split across three to five times as many games, so the odds for a late-2026 or 2027 launch are very likely worse than those figures (and part of the count is a tagging artefact, so the true change is uncertain).
- Players of this genre review and buy generously compared with Steam overall (median 75 reviews versus 11), so a competent entry has a real floor; but in the bullet-heaven cohort the under-$5 band did markedly worse than $5 to $9.99, which argues against ultra-cheap pricing as a substitute for quality.
- Tagging Gatewarden as tower defense alone would put it in a tag with ordinary-to-weak odds (3.3% reaching 1,000 reviews); the bullet-heaven audience is the stronger one to address first.

### Gaps
- I found no GameDiscoverCo article with genre-specific release counts or medians for bullet heaven or roguelite tower defense; their detailed genre data appears to be behind the Plus/Pro paywall.

## 3. What hooks, presentation, pricing and content did the successful small-team titles have, and what do the failures get criticised for?

### Takeaway
The successful small-team games paired a one-sentence hook or a strong borrowed identity with a distinctive, coherent look, a long-running public demo and creator coverage; analysts say novelty is optional but execution and style are not, and that players quickly spot "reskinned Brotato or Vampire Survivors".

### Cited Findings
- Vital Shell succeeded on "execution and style" rather than novelty: mecha theme with a PS1-era look, licensed music from artists the developer approached with trailers, a demo launched about 3 months after the Steam page and 2 months before Next Fest, a demo trailer that added 2,264 wishlists, and seven days in Popular Upcoming that added 6,652. Marketing metrics "rarely rose above Silver Tier" and streamer outreach got "barely any responses"; the result came from post-launch word of mouth and a bundle offered by the Vampire Survivors team. — [How To Market A Game, 19 May 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/19/2026-state-of-bullet-heavens-how-vital-shell-succeeded/)
- The same article observes that there are many 2D sprite-based games that look like reskinned Brotato or Vampire Survivors and that players seem to detect this (snippet only for the exact wording). — [How To Market A Game, 19 May 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/19/2026-state-of-bullet-heavens-how-vital-shell-succeeded/)
- Gnomes (roguelike tower defense): the hook is that gnomes are towers that can be moved after each wave, plus random maps, relics and a crop adjacency system, described as "unique but not ostentatious"; pixel art with a clear character focus. What worked: Steam page up 8 months before launch, demo up 5 months before launch and left live, more than 1,000 individual emails to creators, Reddit posts in r/roguelites and r/TowerDefense, coverage by Splattercat and Wonderbots, Next Fest (~2,000 wishlists), a Daily Deal and bundles. The team shipped a full 1.0 to qualify for New & Trending and earned $10,000 in the first 24 hours. — [How To Market A Game, 13 Aug 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/08/13/gnomes-tower-defense-with-10-month-dev-time-hits-367484/)
- Gnomes developer advice: "If you have a hard time getting playtesters for a free game you will have a hard time when it is a paid game"; build identity around a deep mechanic genre fans recognise, not a gimmick. — [How To Market A Game, 13 Aug 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/08/13/gnomes-tower-defense-with-10-month-dev-time-hits-367484/)
- Backpack Battles: a legible hook (inventory Tetris + asynchronous auto-battler), a free demo updated with about 38 weekly patches before launch, early Japanese localization that drew hololive VTubers, community-suggested Chinese New Year content, and new key art that was well received in Asia. — [GameDiscoverCo, 24 Apr 2024](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies)
- Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor's result is attributed to making a spin-off of an existing loved IP in a hot genre. — [How To Market A Game, 8 Apr 2024](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/04/08/how-creating-a-spinoff-for-a-hot-genre-lead-to-success-for-deep-rock-galactic-survivor/)
- The King is Watching: publisher credits the roguelite pivot for replayability, plus heavy Chinese localization and marketing. — [gamedev.net, 6 Mar 2026](https://gamedev.net/news/1997-how-steam-changes-and-a-china-strategy-helped-tinybuilds-the-king-is-watching/)
- Timing argument from a marketing piece: many early survivors-likes did well because they caught the wave within months of Vampire Survivors; that window has closed (snippet only). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/how-to-get-attention-for-your-indie-game-in-a-crowded-market)
- Review scores of the weaker comparables (Steam data, 27 Nov 2026): Monsters are Coming! 78%, Swarm Grinder 76%, Isle of Arrows 79%, The Bazaar 66%, versus 94% to 98% for Vampire Survivors, Brotato, Halls of Torment, Thronefall, Vital Shell and Megabonk. — [Monsters are Coming!](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2934220); [Swarm Grinder](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1375900); [Isle of Arrows](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1946970); [The Bazaar](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1617400)

### Inferences
- Each breakout can be described in one line that also shows in a screenshot: "Diablo 1 as a survivors game" (Halls of Torment), "minimalist kingdom defence you can play in 15 minutes" (Thronefall), "your subjects only work where you look" (The King is Watching), "3D survivors with movement tech" (Megabonk), "mecha survivors that looks like a PS1 game" (Vital Shell). Gatewarden currently has a feature list (hero + gate + cards + army + shrines) rather than a single visual hook; "one hero and a merged army hold a gate against hordes from every side" needs to be visible in the capsule and first seconds of the trailer.
- The small-team successes mostly had a specific retro or stylised look chosen on purpose (pixel art, PS1 3D, low-poly minimalism). None of the sourced successes relied on generic flat vector art with synthesized sound, so presentation is where Gatewarden is furthest from the reference set.
- In this sample, sub-80% review scores coincide with mid-tier outcomes even with a publisher (Monsters are Coming!), so launch-quality polish and balance matter more than feature count.

### Gaps
- I did not read Steam negative reviews or Reddit threads directly, so I have no sourced, quoted list of typical complaints (lack of content, generic look, weak audio, no identity) for failed titles. The only sourced statement on this is the How To Market A Game remark about reskins. This question is only partly answered.
- No failed-survivors-like postmortem with numbers was found in my searches.
- Trailer and capsule specifics for the comparables were not researched.
- Content amounts at launch (number of characters, maps, hours) were not collected for the comparables.

## 4. How do players react to simple vector art, synthesized audio, AI-assisted content and browser-origin ports, and what does Steam require for AI disclosure?

### Takeaway
Steam requires disclosure of AI-generated content that ships in the game and is consumed by players (art, sound, narrative, localization, store marketing), and the disclosure text is shown on the store page; one 2026 study of about 10,000 releases associates the label with a sales penalty, while a GameDiscoverCo survey found only a minority of players reject such games outright. I found no reliable data on reactions to vector art, synthesized audio or browser ports specifically.

### Cited Findings
- Valve's AI disclosure form (in place since early 2024, wording revised January 2026) states: "Efficiency gains through the use of these tools is not the focus of this section. Instead, it is concerned with the use of AI in creating content that ships with your game, and is consumed by players. This includes content such as artwork, sound, narrative, localization, etc." It has separate sections for pre-generated and live-generated content, covers marketing materials on the store page, and requires a written statement that is displayed on the Steam store page. AI tools used only in the development environment are not the focus. — [GamingOnLinux, 17 Jan 2026](https://gamingonlinux.com/2026/01/valve-tweak-steam-ai-disclosure-form-for-developers-to-clarify-its-for-content-consumed-by-players); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/software/ai/steam-updates-ai-disclosure-form-to-specify-that-its-focused-on-ai-generated-content-that-is-consumed-by-players-not-efficiency-tools-used-behind-the-scenes/)
- Game Oracle study (Ross Burton, PhD), reported June 2026, covering nearly 10,000 Steam releases: about 21% of 2025 Steam releases carried an AI disclosure, and the label was associated with a "consistent sales penalty". The label does not distinguish between uses, so AI translation is flagged the same way as AI character art. — [GAMES.GG, 24 Jun 2026](https://games.gg/news/ai-content-labels-killing-game-sales/)
- Other coverage of the same study reports roughly 53% fewer user reviews for disclosing games than comparable non-disclosing titles, using review counts as a sales proxy (snippet only; I could not open the article). — [Digital Citizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/steam-games-that-disclose-ai-use-may-sell-far-less-new-study-finds/)
- A GameDiscoverCo-linked survey found 31% of respondents react negatively to AI in games but only 8% would never buy a game because of it; respondents skew more engaged than the average Steam user (snippet only). — [Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/only-8-of-steam-users-say-they-wouldnt-buy-a-game-if-it-contained-ai-1745073/)
- A Reddit analysis reported that almost half of Steam's October 2026 releases disclosed AI use (snippet only). — [PowerUp Gaming, 2 Nov 2026](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/11/02/almost-half-of-steams-october-releases-disclosed-ai-use/)
- A PC Guide poll found many PC gamers believe developers hide AI use despite the disclaimers (snippet only). — [PC Guide](https://www.pcguide.com/news/pc-gamers-remain-skeptical-of-steams-ai-disclaimers-poll-shows-many-believe-game-devs-are-hiding-it/)
- Tim Sweeney's claim that the labels harm developers is reported as unsupported by verified sales data (snippet only). — [tech-insider.org](https://tech-insider.org/tim-sweeney-steam-ai-disclosure-labels-abhorrent-no-sales-data-2026/)
- Browser-origin precedent: Vampire Survivors began as an itch.io release with minimal art and was priced low on Steam partly for that reason (snippet only). — [Wikiwand: Vampire Survivors](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Vampire_Survivors)

### Inferences
- Under the January 2026 wording, AI used as a coding assistant looks like an "efficiency" use rather than player-consumed content, but the sources do not state how code is treated; AI-generated art, audio, text, capsule art or screenshots clearly must be disclosed. If any of Gatewarden's shipped art, text, icons or store capsules were AI-generated, the owner must disclose it and should word the statement narrowly and specifically (the repository already has an "AI disclosure draft" in the store page copy).
- The Game Oracle result is a correlation: games that disclose AI may also be lower-effort on average, so the penalty attributable to the label itself is uncertain. With disclosure approaching half of new releases by late 2026, the label is becoming less of a differentiator, but vocal hostility remains.
- The larger risk for Gatewarden is probably not the label but being perceived as generic or low-effort because of flat vector art and synthesized audio in a genre where reviewers already call out reskins.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable sources on how Steam players react to flat vector art or to synthesized/procedural audio in this genre. This is a gap, not evidence that it is fine.
- I found no data on browser/HTML5 or Electron games ported to Steam beyond the Vampire Survivors origin story.
- The primary Game Oracle report was not located; figures come from secondary coverage, and its methodology (controls, comparison set) is not described in what I could read.
- I did not read Valve's Steamworks documentation page directly; the form wording is quoted via GamingOnLinux.

## 5. What price and content amount are typical at launch, and is Early Access commonly used?

### Takeaway
The median bullet-heaven list price is $5.99, the $5 to $9.99 band outperforms under-$5, and solo-developer hits cluster at $4.99 to $9.99 while hybrids with more systems or a publisher sit at $12.99 to $19.99; Early Access was used by most of the big comparables, but most EA games earn less at 1.0 than at EA launch and a 1.0 launch is what triggers New & Trending.

### Cited Findings
- Median list price of paid Bullet Heaven games (2024 to mid-2025 cohort): $5.99, versus $4.99 for Steam overall; $5 to $9.99 games had a median of 141 reviews versus 37 for under-$5 games. — [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/bullet-heaven)
- Current list prices of solo or two-person comparables: Vampire Survivors $4.99, Brotato $4.99, 20 Minutes Till Dawn $4.99, Just King $4.99, Vital Shell $5.99, Halls of Torment $6.66, Megabonk $9.99, Tiny Rogues $9.99, Thronefall $12.99, Backpack Battles $14.99; roguelite tower-defense titles: Rogue Tower $14.99, Tower Dominion $17.49, Nordhold $19.99, Emberward $19.99 (Steam data, 27 Nov 2026). — [Brotato](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1942280); [Vital Shell](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3741860); [Megabonk](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3405340); [Thronefall](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2239150); [Nordhold](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3028310)
- Early Access use among the comparables: Vampire Survivors, Brotato, Halls of Torment, DRG: Survivor, Soulstone Survivors, 20 Minutes Till Dawn, Thronefall, Backpack Battles, Emberward and Yet Another Zombie Survivors all went through Early Access; Megabonk, Vital Shell, Gnomes, The King is Watching and Monsters are Coming! launched as 1.0. — [Thronefall coverage](https://www.gamesmarket.global/indie-hit-thronefall-sells-over-one-million-copies-588b03986d04328e71544cbcb1d1b454/); [GameDiscoverCo on Backpack Battles](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies); [HTMAG on Gnomes](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/08/13/gnomes-tower-defense-with-10-month-dev-time-hits-367484/); [HTMAG on Vital Shell](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/19/2026-state-of-bullet-heavens-how-vital-shell-succeeded/)
- Early Access share of all Steam releases: 10.4% in 2024, 11.3% in 2025, 13.7% in 2026 to mid-October (all genres, snippet only). — [tech-insider.org](https://tech-insider.org/early-access-13-7-percent-steam-releases-players-expect-launch-2026/)
- GameDiscoverCo: of 225 Steam games leaving Early Access in 2025, only 20% (45) did better at 1.0 than at their Early Access launch; median 1.0-month revenue was about 40% of the first Early Access month (snippet only). — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-state-of-steam-early-access-graduates)
- The Gnomes team chose a full 1.0 launch to avoid ongoing support obligations and to qualify for New & Trending, which is withheld until 1.0. — [How To Market A Game, 13 Aug 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/08/13/gnomes-tower-defense-with-10-month-dev-time-hits-367484/)
- Development time benchmarks for small-team successes: Gnomes about 10 months (2 people); Vital Shell about 15 months (solo, part-time); Zukowski suggests about a year as the minimum to reach the current quality bar. — [HTMAG on Gnomes](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/08/13/gnomes-tower-defense-with-10-month-dev-time-hits-367484/); [HTMAG on Vital Shell](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/19/2026-state-of-bullet-heavens-how-vital-shell-succeeded/)

### Inferences
- For a game of Gatewarden's scope (4 heroes, 3 maps, 20 waves) with vector art and synth audio, the evidence points to the $4.99 to $6.99 range: it matches Vital Shell, Brotato and the genre median, and avoids direct comparison with $12.99 to $19.99 titles that have far more content and production value. Going below $5 is not supported by the cohort data.
- Early Access is the norm for the biggest survivors-likes but it worked for them because they had an audience at EA launch; for a small game that is already "finished and balanced", a direct 1.0 (the Gnomes and Vital Shell route) keeps the single New & Trending shot and avoids an EA launch that GameDiscoverCo's data suggests is the bigger revenue moment.

### Gaps
- No source gave a typical content amount at launch (characters, maps, hours) for the genre; I cannot say whether 4 heroes and 3 maps is below the norm, though it is well below what Vampire Survivors and Brotato offer today.
- No genre-specific Early Access share was found.
- Launch prices and launch discounts for most comparables were not confirmed.

## 6. Does a free web demo or a Steam demo help such games?

### Takeaway
A Steam demo launched months before release is consistently reported as helpful, mainly by generating wishlists rather than direct sales, and the best small-team examples kept their demos live and updated; I found no data on itch.io or own-website demos beyond anecdotes.

### Cited Findings
- Demos increase sales for most indie games indirectly via wishlists; they can hurt when the build is unpolished or launched within weeks of release. Demo reviews are permanent. — [SteamPageAnalyzer blog](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/do-steam-demos-increase-sales)
- Games that released their demo months before Next Fest earned roughly 2.5x the median wishlists of games that launched the demo during the fest (Zukowski surveys, self-reported). — [SteamPageAnalyzer blog](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/do-steam-demos-increase-sales)
- Median Next Fest wishlist gain: 884 (Jun 2024), 916 (Oct 2024), 1,079 (Feb 2025), 806 (Feb 2026). By pre-fest wishlist tier (Feb 2026, 182 games): under 1,000 pre-fest earned a median of 322; 1,000 to 9,999 earned 1,006; 10,000 to 99,999 earned 5,215. Pre-fest wishlists correlate with fest gains at r = 0.825. — [SteamPageAnalyzer blog](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/do-steam-demos-increase-sales)
- Demo-play-to-wishlist conversion: median 16.33% (Feb 2026 survey) and 19.3% (June 2026 survey). — [SteamPageAnalyzer blog](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/do-steam-demos-increase-sales)
- Wishlist-to-sales: week-one sales are a median of about 10.5% of launch wishlists (GameDiscoverCo, Dec 2024, ~420 games), so 1,000 wishlists is roughly 105 first-week sales. — [SteamPageAnalyzer blog](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/do-steam-demos-increase-sales)
- Backpack Battles ran a free Steam demo from June 2023 with about 38 weekly content patches; the demo peaked at 18,960 concurrent players before the March 2024 Early Access launch that sold 640k in a month. — [GameDiscoverCo, 24 Apr 2024](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-backpack-battles-sold-650k-copies)
- Gnomes released its demo five months before launch and kept it live afterwards; the developer said players often decided to buy after about five minutes; Next Fest added about 2,000 wishlists. — [How To Market A Game, 13 Aug 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/08/13/gnomes-tower-defense-with-10-month-dev-time-hits-367484/)
- Vital Shell's demo had a median play time of 22 minutes, below the 38+ minutes Zukowski associates with strong games, yet the game still reached 17,826 launch wishlists; Next Fest contributed 2,187. — [How To Market A Game, 19 May 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/19/2026-state-of-bullet-heavens-how-vital-shell-succeeded/)
- Dome Keeper gained about 40,000 wishlists in a month around its demo (single anecdote, Zukowski 2023). — [SteamPageAnalyzer blog](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/do-steam-demos-increase-sales)
- Vampire Survivors' free itch.io version drew little engagement on its own; the Steam release and YouTube coverage drove the breakout (snippet only). — [Wikiwand: Vampire Survivors](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Vampire_Survivors)

### Inferences
- Gatewarden's existing browser build is best used as a zero-friction playtest and creator-outreach tool (the Gnomes advice about finding free playtesters applies), but the measurable benefit comes from a Steam demo tied to the store page, launched at least a few months before release and before a Next Fest, because that is what converts into wishlists.
- At the typical ~10% week-one conversion, a launch with a few thousand wishlists implies only a few hundred first-week sales; Gnomes treated about 7,000 wishlists as the minimum to launch and Vital Shell launched with about 17,800.

### Gaps
- No quantitative evidence on itch.io or own-website web demos helping (or hurting) Steam sales was found; whether a free full web version cannibalises Steam sales is unanswered.
- The demo statistics are developer self-reports collected by How To Market A Game and relayed by a secondary blog; I did not open the original survey posts.
